## Common misreadings to revisit
> The perennial traps in this subject (from `the-physical-prior.md`). The tutor watches
> for these, notes when one comes up, and revisits later to make sure it has stuck.

- "a PINN is a faster PDE solver" — no; FEM/FDM win on well-posed **forward** problems; PINNs earn their keep on **inverse / incomplete-model / mesh-free** problems
- "modal synthesis = additive synthesis" — the sinusoids come from an **eigenproblem** with constrained `f_k, d_k, a_k`
- "waveguide and modal are different physics" — **same linear system**, different coordinates (traveling vs standing waves)
- "more modes = better" — cost `O(N)`; perceptual relevance; amplitude culling
- "a physics prior removes the need for data" — it **reduces** it; the network still fits excitation/residual, and a wrong prior fits garbage
- "damping is as predictable as frequency" — frequency from `E, ρ`, geometry; **damping is measured per mode**
- "inharmonic partials mean the model is wrong" — stiffness stretch **is** the physics
- "the differentiable modal fit finds the true modes" — it finds **a** matching bank (many-to-one); initialize from measured peaks
