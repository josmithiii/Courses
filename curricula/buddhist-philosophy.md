# Buddhist Philosophy

**Status:** 🟡 Partial — course 1 is active; course 2 is planned.

## Goal

Build the doctrinal foundations of Buddhist philosophy in historical order: first the
**early (pre-Mahāyāna)** view from the Pali Canon, then the **Mahāyāna** development
that builds on and reinterprets it (emptiness, Madhyamaka, Yogācāra, Buddha-nature).
Philosophy only — practice and devotional material are out of scope.

**Who it's for.** Anyone wanting the *philosophical* core of Buddhism, doctrine by
doctrine, with primary texts read directly. No prior Buddhist study assumed; course 1
is the entry point and has no prerequisite.

## Prerequisite

None. [`../buddhism-early-philosophy/`](../buddhism-early-philosophy/) is the entry course.

## Through-line

Both courses pin abstract doctrine to a **10-second moment of ordinary experience**:
course 1 reads the early analysis through it; course 2 re-reads the *same* moment through
the Mahāyāna lens of emptiness — showing the two views as successive readings of one
experience rather than disconnected systems.

## Source of truth

- **Early:** the Pali Canon, with named suttas read on [SuttaCentral](https://suttacentral.net/).
- **Mahāyāna:** the Prajñāpāramitā sūtras (Heart, Diamond), Nāgārjuna's *Mūlamadhyamakakārikā*,
  and core Yogācāra / Buddha-nature texts (to be pinned when course 2 is authored).
