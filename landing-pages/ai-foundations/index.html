AI track · start here · free

From one neuron to a modern language model.

A beginner's course in how today's AI actually works: neural networks, PyTorch, image models, Transformers, large language models and diffusion. An AI tutor teaches one idea at a time, checks it with a small exercise, and remembers where you left off.

New to machine learning, new to Python, math a bit rusty: all fine. Python and the math are taught as each idea needs them.

The route

Eight phases, each ending in something you build

One concept per lesson, about an hour a day. It's fine to spend several days on one topic; the course tracks where you really are.

  1. 0Orientation. What AI, machine learning and deep learning are. Python set up, with a two-lesson crash start.
  2. 1Learning from data. The neuron, activation functions, the multilayer perceptron, loss, gradient descent, backpropagation, the training loop.
  3. 2PyTorch for real. Tensors, autograd, your own training loop, overfitting and how to prevent it. Build: a handwritten-digit classifier.
  4. 3Image models. Convolution, feature maps, pooling, transfer learning. Build: a small CNN.
  5. 4Sequences and Transformers. Embeddings, attention, self-attention, the Transformer block. Build: a tiny character-level Transformer.
  6. 5Large language models. Tokens, next-token prediction, pretraining and fine-tuning, RLHF, sampling. Build: run a small pretrained LLM.
  7. 6Diffusion. VAEs and GANs in brief, then learning to undo noise, and text-to-image. Build: generate images with a small diffusion model.
  8. 7Putting it together. How modern systems combine the pieces, a capstone project of your choice, and where to go next.
A session

Picture first, then a small exercise

Each session recaps the last idea and asks one recall question. Then it introduces one new idea with an analogy or a picture before any notation or code, and doesn't move on until a small exercise shows it has landed. You type and run the code yourself, and the tutor explains each line.

Your progress and a log of each session stay in a folder on your own computer.
