Graduate research · Stanford Music 423

Research tracks in AI for music and audio.

For graduate students who want to read the primary literature and do research in one or more lineages: neural audio generation, language and sequence models, and differentiable and physical audio DSP. Each track is a sequence of tutored courses that teach from the Music 423 paper collection and link back into its papers.

The collection has about 300 papers in about 25 topic areas, each with an interlinked wiki. Pick one track, or run two side by side.

The collection

Read the papers, not just the summaries

Every course teaches from a topic wiki in the Music 423 collection and links back into its PDFs, so a learner on any track ends up able to open any topic and read it. New papers are added as the field moves; in September 2026, 41 papers from DAFx26 joined, most of them in Track C.

Browse it

The collection on GitLab: the topic directories, grouped by track, each with its papers and wiki.

The interactive knowledge graph: every wiki page and paper as a node, colored by topic, with author and evolution views.

Get started

One line to install every course

Install Claude Code (it runs in a terminal and in the Claude desktop app's Code tab, and needs a Claude account), then paste this into a terminal, once:

claude plugin marketplace add https://github.com/josmithiii/Courses && claude plugin install courses@josmithiii-courses

Then start a session in any folder and ask for a course by name, for example let's do today's audio-codecs lesson.

The courses are free and open. The tutor runs on your own Claude account, and your progress stays in a folder on your own computer.
