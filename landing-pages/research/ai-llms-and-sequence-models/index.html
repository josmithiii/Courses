Research Track B · in progress

From attention to the models after the Transformer.

The general AI backbone: the Transformer and its LLM descendants, the long-context and efficiency responses (linear attention, state-space models, Mamba, RWKV, hybrids), and the post-training that turns a language model into an assistant.

For anyone who wants the backbone itself, or an audio researcher who wants to read the codec-LM and DiT papers of Track A as an insider.

Before you start

What the track assumes

AI Foundations or equivalent; its phases on attention, Transformers and LLMs are where this track starts.

Get started

One line to install every course

Install Claude Code (it runs in a terminal and in the Claude desktop app's Code tab, and needs a Claude account), then paste this into a terminal, once:

claude plugin marketplace add https://github.com/josmithiii/Courses && claude plugin install courses@josmithiii-courses

Then start a session in any folder and say let's do today's ai-miracle-decade-plus lesson, or name any course on this page.

The courses are free and open, and the full track plan is in the LLMs & Sequence Models curriculum. The tutor runs on your own Claude account, and your progress stays in a folder on your own computer.
