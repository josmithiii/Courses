Research Track A · complete

How modern music and audio generators work.

From raw waveforms to the tokens and latents every audio generator consumes, then both ways of generating from them: language-modeling codec tokens, and diffusion in a continuous latent. It ends at 2024-2026 systems such as Stable Audio, MusicGen, DiffRhythm and ACE-Step.

For learners who know modern deep learning in general and want the audio-generation specialization. No DSP background is assumed; the first course builds the audio intuition it needs.

Before you start

What the track assumes

AI Foundations or equivalent: MLPs, backprop, PyTorch, CNNs, attention and Transformers, LLMs and basic diffusion. That is exactly where the first course starts. No signal processing is required; the book courses are there for anyone who wants the DSP underneath.

The courses

Three core courses, split at the field's main fork

The first course ends at the fork that organizes the whole field: discrete tokens or a continuous latent. Courses 2 and 3 take one branch each.

Course 1 · audio-codecs

Audio Codecs

Brisk DSP (sampling, STFT, mel), then autoencoders, VQ-VAE, residual vector quantization, and SoundStream, EnCodec and DAC, through semantic vs acoustic tokens and evaluation.

The fork: discrete residual-VQ tokens or a continuous VAE latent.

~20 sessions
Course on GitHub
Course 2 · audio-codec-lms

Codec Language Models

The discrete-token branch: generate audio by language-modeling codec tokens, from WaveNet and Jukebox through AudioLM, MusicLM and MusicGen to masked models (VampNet, MAGNeT) and VALL-E / MIDI-VALLE.

The RVQ token grid: what a model predicts, and in what order.

~25 sessions
Course on GitHub
Course 3 · audio-diffusion-dit

Audio Diffusion and DiTs

The continuous-latent branch: latent diffusion (AudioLDM, Tango), the diffusion transformer, Stable Audio and the U-Net-to-DiT swap, rectified-flow MM-DiT (FluxMusic), DiffRhythm and ACE-Step.

The latent canvas: generation as denoising a continuous VAE latent.

~25 sessions

neural-audio-resynthesis

Neural Audio Resynthesis

Creating and editing audio with neural methods: an encode, steer, decode loop that combines a self-supervised conditioning latent, a disentangling bias and a rectified-flow DiT decoder, ending on the problem of evaluating without a reference.

The latent is expressive but illegible; the research game is winning back legible control.

Get started

One line to install every course

Install Claude Code (it runs in a terminal and in the Claude desktop app's Code tab, and needs a Claude account), then paste this into a terminal, once:

claude plugin marketplace add https://github.com/josmithiii/Courses && claude plugin install courses@josmithiii-courses

Then start a session in any folder and say let's do today's audio-codecs lesson, or name any course on this page.

The courses are free and open, and the full track plan is in the AI Music & Audio curriculum. The tutor runs on your own Claude account, and your progress stays in a folder on your own computer.
